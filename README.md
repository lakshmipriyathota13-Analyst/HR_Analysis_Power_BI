# HR Analytics Dashboard 📊

## Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition, performance, and training patterns using **Power BI**.  
The dashboard provides actionable insights for HR teams to improve retention, compensation strategies, and workforce planning.

---

## 📂 Dataset
- Source: HR_Analytics.csv  
- Key fields: Age, Department, Attrition, Monthly Income, Salary Slab, Performance Rating, Training Times Last Year, Work-Life Balance, Years at Company, Gender, Education Field, Business Travel.

---

## 🔑 KPIs
- Attrition Rate (%)  
- Average Monthly Income  
- Average Age  
- Average Years at Company  
- Overtime %  
- Gender Ratio (Male vs Female)  
- Training Times per Year

## 📌 Insights
- Attrition varies significantly by department and job role.  
- Salary slabs and overtime correlate with higher attrition.  
- Training frequency impacts performance ratings and retention.  
- Work-life balance is a strong predictor of employee satisfaction.
